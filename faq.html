Help & FAQ

Are these official shirts?

No. KitVault sells unofficial replica football shirts. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed by any club, federation, or player. See our Terms of Service for details.

Why are your prices so low?

We keep costs down by selling unofficial replicas rather than club-licensed merchandise, which is what lets us offer prices well below official retailers.

How do I know what size to order?

Check our Size Guide for a full chest and length breakdown by size. If you're between sizes, we'd generally suggest sizing up.

How long does delivery take?

See our Shipping & Returns page for current delivery estimates and how to arrange a return or exchange.

Can I return a shirt?

Yes — unworn, unwashed shirts in original condition can be returned within 30 days. Full details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
