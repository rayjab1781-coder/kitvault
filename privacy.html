Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information needed to fulfil it — your name, email address, shipping address, and order details. We don't collect or store payment card details ourselves; that's handled securely by our payment provider.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order or respond to enquiries
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations

Sharing

We share order information with the couriers and payment providers needed to fulfil your order. We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies to remember your cart and preferences while you shop. It does not use cookies for third-party advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain certain records.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, email KitVaultCustomerService@gmail.com.
