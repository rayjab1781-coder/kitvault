Sizing

Size Guide

All KitVault shirts are cut true to standard replica sizing. If you're between sizes, or prefer a looser matchday fit, we'd generally suggest sizing up.

How to measure

  • Chest: measure straight across underarm to underarm on a shirt that fits you well, then double it.
  • Length: measure from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.
